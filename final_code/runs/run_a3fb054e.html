Root Causes
Primary — Verbosity + Repetitive Clarification Loops
The therapist repeatedly re-asks variants of the same question across multiple turns. Turns 7, 9, 11, 13, and 15 all attempt to get the patient to specify the same thing (the situational trigger → the automatic thought). Each time the patient provides an answer, the therapist's next response acknowledges it and then asks yet another refinement. A competent response would treat the patient's first clear answer as sufficient and move on.
Primary — Restarting When New Content Appears
When the patient introduces new worry content (texting anxiety on turn 22), the therapist treats it as a new presenting problem requiring full clarification from scratch, rather than recognizing it as a variant of the same catastrophizing pattern already identified. Turn 23 literally begins: "Before we design any experiment, I want to make sure we're crystal clear…" — undoing the progress of the previous 4 turns.
Secondary — Protocol Over Presence
Scored 3/6 on Understanding. When the patient shares emotional distress, the therapist tends to respond procedurally ("let me nail down one concrete detail") rather than first acknowledging the feeling. This creates a mechanical quality that reduces genuine collaboration and feels more like an intake form than therapy.
Secondary — No Session Awareness
The therapist never acknowledges the turn budget or signals urgency as the session approaches its limit. A human therapist would start managing toward closure around turn 16–18. Instead the therapist continues opening new threads right up to the cutoff.
